🎯 Executive Overview

The Intelligent Data Dictionary Agent is an enterprise-grade AI platform that automatically extracts, analyzes, and documents database schemas while providing real-time data quality insights and intelligent conversational assistance.

What is the Intelligent Data Dictionary Agent?

In today's data-driven world, organizations struggle with undocumented databases, tribal knowledge, and poor data governance. Our AI-powered platform solves this by automatically connecting to your databases, extracting complete metadata, analyzing data quality in real-time, generating human-readable documentation with AI-powered business context, and providing a conversational interface for instant answers to any database-related question.


Built for: Data Engineers, Database Administrators, Data Analysts, Business Intelligence Teams, Compliance Officers, and anyone who needs to understand their organization's data landscape quickly and accurately.
